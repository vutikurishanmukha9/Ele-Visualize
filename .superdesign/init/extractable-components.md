# Extractable Components

The following components from the codebase are ideal for reuse across Superdesign canvases and designs:

1. **`BohrModel3D`** (`src/components/BohrModel3D.tsx`)
   - 3D interactive subatomic hologram preview with customizable shells and colors.
2. **`ElementCard`** (`src/components/ElementCard.tsx`)
   - Standard scientific element tile with category borders and hover effects.
3. **`QuantumNumbersHUD`** (`src/components/QuantumNumbersHUD.tsx`)
   - Quantum mechanical state readout for principal, azimuthal, magnetic, and spin quantum numbers.
4. **`SpectroscopyBar`** (`src/components/SpectroscopyBar.tsx`)
   - Colorimetric physical optical spectrum bar with discrete spectral lines.
5. **`ThermalScrubber`** (`src/components/ThermalScrubber.tsx`)
   - Interactive Kelvin/Celsius thermodynamic phase transition slider.
